[h1]Wil's Better Beds[/h1]
[i]Smarter beds. Happier colonists.[/i]

Where your colonists sleep now matters.

Colonists choose beds based on comfort and happiness, negotiate for better bedrooms, and consider who's sleeping nearby. Couples look for rooms together, royalty expects proper accommodations, and even rivalries can affect a good night's rest.

[b]And you always have the final say.[/b]

[h2]How It Works[/h2]

Wil's Better Beds uses RimWorld's own [b]mood point system[/b] to evaluate sleeping arrangements.

Colonists only seek a better room when their current sleeping situation causes a penalty. If they're content where they are, they'll stay put.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[h3]Smarter Bed Selection[/h3]
[list]
[*] Bedless colonists choose the [b]best available bed[/b], not simply the closest.
[*] Unhappy sleepers automatically move to free beds that offer a meaningful improvement.
[*] Bedroom quality, rest effectiveness, and temperature affect their decisions.
[/list]

[h3]Royalty & Personal Preferences[/h3]
[list]
[*] Royal colonists expect bedrooms appropriate to their titles.
[*] Jealous colonists envy significantly nicer rooms.
[*] Greedy colonists want an impressive bedroom, while Ascetics prefer a humble one.
[*] Bedrooms that are too hot or cold count against comfort.
[/list]

[h3]Relationships Matter[/h3]

Colonists care about who sleeps nearby. Within a configurable radius (default [b]35 tiles[/b]), relationships and shared identities affect their mood.

[list]
[*] Disliked relatives: [b]-2 mood[/b]
[*] Disliked colonists: [b]-4 mood[/b]
[*] Nearby occupied prisoner beds: [b]-3 mood[/b]
[*] Shared ideoligion: [b]+2 mood[/b]
[*] Shared xenotype: [b]+2 mood[/b]
[/list]

These are actual mood thoughts, not hidden modifiers. Every value can be adjusted in the settings.

[h3]Negotiate for a Better Bedroom[/h3]

Unhappy colonists can spend recreation time convincing someone to give up their room.

[list]
[*] Negotiations use a [b]contested d20 roll[/b].
[*] Social skill, relationships, royal titles, and colony seniority influence the outcome.
[*] Stronger colonists may use Melee skill to intimidate.
[*] Winners gain Social experience.
[*] Negotiations and intimidation can have lasting mood consequences.
[/list]

Not every conversation ends peacefully. Some colonists give up their rooms willingly; others remember being pushed around.

[h3]Couples Stay Together[/h3]
[list]
[*] Couples can negotiate for a bedroom together.
[*] The partner with the stronger Social skill handles negotiations.
[*] If only one partner wants to move, they first convince the other.
[*] Couples can automatically claim a suitable empty room without evicting anyone.
[/list]

[h3]You're Still in Control[/h3]
[list]
[*] Use the vanilla [b]Set Owner[/b] button to lock a bed.
[*] Locked beds cannot be taken through negotiation.
[*] Their owners won't automatically move or negotiate for another room.
[/list]

[h3]Room Management[/h3]

The [b]Assign Rooms[/b] button on the Assign tab provides an overview of your colony's bedrooms.

[list]
[*] View rooms, occupied beds, scores, sizes, and owners.
[*] Hover to highlight rooms on the map.
[*] Double-click to jump directly to a room.
[*] Assign owners room by room.
[/list]

[h3]No Bed? Find a Chair![/h3]

Colonists, guests, and visitors without a bed can doze in an available indoor chair rather than sleeping on the floor.

It's still not ideal, but it's better than the ground.

[h2]Mod Compatibility[/h2]

[b]Hospitality[/b]
[list]
[*] Guests retain Hospitality's bed selection, rental system, and rules.
[*] Guests without available beds may sleep in chairs.
[/list]

[b]Rimsential - Spaceports & Trade Caravans[/b]
[list]
[*] Eligible visitors can rent available guest beds using their silver.
[*] Visitors who cannot afford a bed can sleep in chairs.
[/list]

[b]Dubs Bad Hygiene[/b]
[list]
[*] Colonists won't interrupt toilet, bathing, or shower activities for negotiations.
[/list]

[b]Interaction Bubbles[/b]
[list]
[*] Bedroom negotiations appear as speech bubbles.
[/list]

[b]Additional Support[/b]
[list]
[*] Dubs Mint Menus
[*] Vanilla Factions Expanded - Empire
[*] Royalty
[*] Ideology
[*] Biotech
[*] Other bed and room mods
[/list]

Bed selection respects the game's existing bed eligibility rules. Individual compatibility patches are isolated, so a broken patch can be skipped and logged without disabling the remaining features.

[h2]Settings & Customization[/h2]

Nearly everything is configurable through the mod settings.

[list]
[*] Individual feature toggles
[*] Mood bonuses and penalties
[*] Relationship and proximity distances
[*] Negotiation rules and cooldowns
[*] Skill and dice-roll modifiers
[/list]

Every setting includes a tooltip explaining its effects.

[h2]Requirements & Load Order[/h2]

[b]Requires:[/b] Harmony

[b]Supported versions:[/b] RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7

[b]Optional DLC:[/b] Royalty, Ideology, and Biotech add their respective bedroom rules but are not required.

Safe to add to an existing save.

Load after Harmony and any bed, room, or guest mods.

[h2]Important Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Prisoners, slaves, children, and medical beds retain vanilla behavior.
[*] Automatic room changes only occur when a colonist has a sleep-related penalty.
[*] Manually locked beds are always respected.
[/list]
